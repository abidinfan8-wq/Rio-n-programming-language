# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

As contributors and maintainers of RIO, we pledge to respect all people who contribute through reporting issues, posting feature requests, updating documentation, submitting pull requests, and other activities.

We follow the Contributor Covenant v2.1. For the full text and examples, see: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

Be kind, be respectful, and be collaborative.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the maintainers at `abidinfan8@gmail.com` with a link to the relevant activity.
